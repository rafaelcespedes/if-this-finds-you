# Code of conduct

If This Finds You is built around real people's letters, written by people who can no longer answer back. We ask the
same care of everyone who takes part here: in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project
gathers.

## What we ask

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people here are trying something new.
- Critique the work, not the person. Be specific, and say what would make it better.
- Make room. Newcomers, people writing in a second language, and people who've never opened a pull request before
  are all welcome.
- Treat the letters and their writers with respect, even the ones whose views we wouldn't share today.

## What isn't welcome

Harassment of any kind; insults and personal attacks; sexualised language or imagery; discriminatory jokes or
language; publishing anyone's private information; and anything else a reasonable person would find unwelcoming.

## If something goes wrong

Tell Rafael Cespedes, the project's maintainer, with a direct message on X ([@rafcespedes](https://x.com/rafcespedes))
or through his site ([rafaelcespedes.com](https://rafaelcespedes.com)). Reports are kept private. Anything that breaks
this code can be edited or removed, and anyone who keeps breaking it can be blocked from the project.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
