## Polish school copperplate (kaligrafia), 1906 — style `polish-1906` ("Polish school 1906", joined)

| Item | Konrad Prószyński (writing as Kazimierz Promyk, 1851–1908), *Wprawy w piękne pisanie piórem, czyli Wzory kaligraficzne przenośne z objaśnieniami i radami dla uczących i uczących się*, 3rd edition, Warsaw: Księgarnia Krajowa Konrada Prószyńskiego, 1906. | Movement | Steep copperplate: the plate's slant guides stand 30° from vertical; the skeletons keep 18° (the writer's slant adds to it). Pointed-pen shading, heavy downstrokes and hairline upstrokes. Small letters join on long diagonal hairlines from the baseline (measured lead-in angle about 40°) that meet the next letter at half the x-height. Ascenders are unlooped: the lead-in hairline climbs to the top and the shaded stem comes back down over it (b d h k l t); descenders loop (g j y), f has a top loop and a crossbar, p has a half-ascender stem and a straight descender. b o w leave from a small knot at the x-height; r has a knotted shoulder (r.2); s is pointed with its base closed by a ball; k has a closed knot on its arm; z is flat-topped. Proportions: x-height 0.55 of the capital height, ascenders reach the capital line, descenders about 0.66. Capitals are oval copperplate forms that start or end in a ball (A B H I K M N P S T Y), with wavy cap strokes on F and T, loops at the base of D and L, looped descenders on G and J; they do not join. Cursive ł has a short flat bar sitting on top of the stem, not across it |
| Note | The plates are ruled: horizontal rules, 60° slant guides and column rules run through every letter, and the hairlines are as thin as the rules, so the automatic skeleton could not separate them. Every letter was drawn as hand-placed centreline points on a rule-cleaned view of the plate, snapped to the ink's centre (stored as `manual` strokes). The ł bar lies on the ruled ascender line, so its full length cannot be told from the rule; the visible part is a short flat head (about 28 px on the plate) that vanished at writing size, so on review the bar was lengthened to about 54 px across the stem top, keeping it on top (never through the stem, which would read as t). The engine's hand sampler currently sets its own x-height and width for non-`script` styles |
