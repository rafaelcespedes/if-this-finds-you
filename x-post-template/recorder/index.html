Record your hands typing

This page writes in ink as you type, like the video will. It keeps your keys, the pointer and the webcam on one clock.

Use Google Chrome for this page: other browsers may not record the camera.
  1. Phone above the keyboard, looking straight down, sideways, with the top of its picture toward the screen. Keyboard, trackpad and both wrists in view. Start it recording now.
  2. Volume up, no headphones. The phone listens for this page's beeps to line up with it.
  3. Turn on the camera and check your face is in the picture.
  4. Press Start. Three beeps, then a higher one: look at the camera for a moment, then look down and write.
  5. Click Finish at the top when you're done. Then stop the phone.

The laptop's camera films your face for the opening.

Writing: ⌫ crosses out, ⌘B goes over, ⌘U underlines, ⌘Z lifts wet ink.
